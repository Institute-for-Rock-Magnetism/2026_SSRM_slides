# 2026 IRM Summer School — Lecture Slides

Slide decks for the 2026 Summer School of Rock Magnetism at the
[Institute for Rock Magnetism](https://cse.umn.edu/irm) (University of
Minnesota). The lectures accompany the
[Duluth Complex group project](https://github.com/Institute-for-Rock-Magnetism/2026_SSRM_Duluth_Complex),
in which we work with drill core D-6A through the week.

**View the slides:**
[institute-for-rock-magnetism.github.io/2026_SSRM_slides](https://institute-for-rock-magnetism.github.io/2026_SSRM_slides/)

## Navigating a deck

- **Arrow keys** move between slides; **Esc** shows the slide overview;
  **f** toggles fullscreen
- Many slides have interactive figures — drag sliders, box-zoom on plots
  (double-click to reset), and hover for details
- For a PDF copy, add `?print-pdf` to the end of a deck's URL and print
  from your browser

Each deck also works offline: download its folder and open `index.html`
directly in a browser.
