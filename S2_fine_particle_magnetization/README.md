# S2 — Fine-particle magnetization: anisotropy, domain states, and hysteresis

Session 2 slide deck for the 2026 IRM Summer School of Rock Magnetism
(Tuesday, July 21: "Fine particle magnetism I" and "II", two hours with a
break). A reveal.js deck in seven parts plus a closing set of extra slides:
(1) electronic spins and a succinct treatment of paramagnetism; (2)
exchange, the magnetic ordering zoo, and the Curie temperature; (3) magnetic
anisotropy energies — magnetocrystalline (with its temperature dependence,
the isotropic point, and the Verwey transition), uniaxial/hematite,
magnetostriction, and shape; (4) the flipping field (Stoner–Wohlfarth); (5)
hysteresis loops; (6) processing the students' D-6A VSM data — paramagnetic
slope correction, Mr/Ms against single-domain theory, and the Day plot as
motivation for multidomain behavior; (7) the departure from single domain
through flower and vortex states framed as an energy-budget competition; and
extra slides on the backfield experiment. Deep multidomain physics is
deliberately excluded — it is covered in Max Brown's "Magnetic domain
states" lecture that follows at 11:00.

Content follows chapters 3, 4, and 5 (through the backfield section,
stopping before FORCs) of Tauxe & Swanson-Hysell (2026), *Essentials of
Paleomagnetism*, interactive JupyterBook edition
([pmagpy.github.io/Essentials-JupyterBook](https://pmagpy.github.io/Essentials-JupyterBook/)).

### Canvas widgets ([js/widgets.js](js/widgets.js))

Five canvas-based widgets with no external dependencies, in the style of the
S1 deck (plot helpers shared with `S1_Duluth_dia_para_ferro/js/widgets.js`).
The Stoner–Wohlfarth widgets implement the exact physics — moments ride the
local minimum of ε/Ku = sin²θ − 2h·cos(θ−φ) and flip only when that minimum
vanishes; nothing is fitted or sketched:

- **Single-grain SW loop** (`data-widget="swsingle"`): field-angle φ slider
  (with 0°/22°/45°/70°/90° presets matching the chapter figure), grain
  cartoon with the moment riding the energy minimum, and the exact loop with
  the switching field μ₀H_f(φ) marked. Reproduces the chapter's loop gallery:
  square at φ = 0°, collapsing to a reversible line at 90°.
- **SD assemblage loop** (`data-widget="sdloop"`, static): major loop and
  initial magnetization curve of 240 uniaxial grains with stratified 3-D
  random easy axes, so Mr/Ms = 0.500 exactly and μ₀Hc ≈ 0.48 μ₀H_K emerge
  from the population (verified against Stoner & Wohlfarth 1948 values).
- **Backfield experiment** (`data-widget="backfield"`): simulates the
  saturate → reverse field → measure-at-zero-field protocol on 400 SW grains
  (each flips at B_K·h_f(ψ), contributing ±cos ψ of remanence). Left panel:
  the emergent switching-field spectrum; right panel: the remanence curve
  against the in-field branch, with Bcr and Bc marked. With no coercivity
  spread, Hcr/Hc = 1.09 — the ideal-SD value; a log-normal spread-of-B_K
  slider broadens the spectrum.
- **Slope correction** (`data-widget="slopecorr"`): hysteresis-processing
  rehearsal — SD magnetite (SW loop, 92 Am²/kg) in a paramagnetic olivine
  matrix; fits the high-field slope at |B| > 0.7 B_max, subtracts it, and
  reads out χ_hf, Ms, Mr, Mr/Ms, and Bc, mirroring the RockmagPy
  `process_hyst_loops` workflow.
- **Domain-state explorer** (`data-widget="domainstate"`): grain-diameter
  slider morphing a schematic spin structure through SD → flower → vortex →
  MD, beside the d³ (self energy of the uniform state) vs. d² (cost of the
  curled state) energy budget. Threshold sizes for equant magnetite follow
  chapter 4 and Nagy et al. (2017); spin fields are schematic 2-D slices.
- **Mrh/Mih decomposition** (`data-widget="mrhmih"`, static): the loop of
  the same SW population split into its irreversible (Mrh = (M⁺−M⁻)/2) and
  reversible (Mih = (M⁺+M⁻)/2) parts with Mr and Brh marked — mirroring
  `rockmag.calc_Mr_Mrh_Mih_Brh` in the Jackson & Solheid (2010) processing
  protocol that RockmagPy implements.

Part 6 follows the RockmagPy `process_hyst_loop` "decision tree"
(gridding/centering/Q, Mrh/Mih, saturation tests, linear vs.
approach-to-saturation high-field corrections after Fabian 2006 and
Jackson & Solheid 2010), matching the `hysteresis_processing_walk_through`
notebook the students use. The extra backfield slides close with the
Stoner–Wohlfarth orientation-population disks (sIRM half-disk and
DC-demagnetized pinwheel — the IRM logo motif, after Bruce Moskowitz's
Summer School lectures), drawn as inline SVG.
